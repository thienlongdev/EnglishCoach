Only English typed phrase headwords, entry definitions, tags and classification
metadata are selected. All examples and third-party quotations, translations,
etymologies and media are excluded. Postprocessed downloads with additional
merged sources are not used. Each retained record attributes its original
en.wiktionary.org entry, whose history supplies contributor attribution.

The derivative shard is identified as wiktionary-cc-by-sa-4.0 and licensed
CC BY-SA 4.0. Transformations: entry/sense selection, normalization, metadata
filtering, question formatting and answer fragments. The shard must be supplied
with its attribution, modification notice and license link when redistributed.
It is not silently folded into the permissive source core.
